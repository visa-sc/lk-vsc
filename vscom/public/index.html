Why Spanish residence applications get refused

A Spanish residence permit is granted only when every formal requirement is met. Refusals do happen, and most of them come down to the same handful of causes.

The most common reasons for a refusal

  • An incomplete or badly assembled file. A single missing certificate, an incorrectly filled form or an outdated template is enough for a negative decision.
  • Documents that do not meet the criteria. Expired certificates, missing apostille, no certified translation into Spanish, or copies submitted instead of originals.
  • Insufficient financial standing. You have to prove convincingly that your income or savings cover you and your family for the whole period.
  • Immigration or criminal record issues. A conviction, a deportation from a Schengen country or a breach of stay rules in Spain will almost certainly close the door.

What to do if your case is complicated

The process looks confusing from the outside. The rules change, the document requirements are strict, and one small mistake costs time, money and sometimes the whole plan to move.

How to do it comfortably

Frequently asked questions

Does this work if I am not in tech?

Yes. The programme fits remote employees, freelancers, sole traders and business owners from any field.

Do I have to work remotely?

No. Remote work is only one of the routes to Spanish residency. For entrepreneurs, financially independent applicants and other situations there are separate programmes, and we will pick the one that fits you.

What are the requirements for an applicant?

A source of income or savings, a degree or work experience, and a clean criminal record certificate. The exact list depends on the programme and we go through it on the free consultation.

Can I apply from Russia or another country?

Yes. We run the whole process remotely from anywhere in the world. You do not need to come to our office.

Do I need to speak Spanish?

No. There is no language requirement for these residence permits.

Will my family move with me?

Yes. Your spouse and children receive their status together with the main applicant.

What taxes does a Spanish resident pay?

It depends on your situation, in particular on how many days a year you actually spend in Spain. We explain the rules and help you understand your obligations after the move.
