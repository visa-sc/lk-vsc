Why applications get refused

  • An incomplete application. One missing certificate or an out-of-date form is enough for a refusal.
  • Documents that don't meet the criteria. Expired certificates, missing apostille, no certified translation, copies instead of originals.
  • Weak proof of income. The money has to be shown the way the authorities expect it, not the way it looks on your bank app.
  • Immigration or criminal history. A conviction or a breach of stay rules almost always closes the door.

Every one of these is avoidable, and every one of them is easier to spot before you start than after a refusal. That's what we go through on the call.

Common questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Free, and with no obligation to go any further.

Do I have to work in tech?

No. Remote employees, freelancers, sole traders and business owners from any field.

Do I need Spanish?

No. There's no language requirement, and the call's in English.

Can I do this from the UK?

Yes, the whole thing runs remotely. Applying from inside Spain gets you the three-year status straight away, and we'll go through that difference on the call.

Does this let me live anywhere in Europe?

Your home is Spain, that's where the status is issued. From there the whole Schengen area opens up: you travel and stay in the other 28 countries with no visas and none of the 90/180 headache Brits have had since Brexit. Moving your permanent home to another EU country is a separate step, and we'll explain how that works on the call.

Will my family come with me?

Yes. Spouse and children take the same route as the main applicant.

What if my plan isn't realistic?

We say so on the call. Cheaper for everyone than finding out later.
